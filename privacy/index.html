Privacy

The apps on this site are personal projects. Each one is used only by its owner, and none of them collect information from anyone else.

food-tracker

  • What it reads: the owner's own Oura daily activity (step count), and the owner's own workout history from connected fitness accounts.
  • Why: to show today's steps and workouts next to the owner's nutrition log.
  • Where it's kept: in the owner's private Google Cloud project. Access credentials are stored in Google Secret Manager.
  • Sharing: none. Data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or anything else.
  • Revoking access: disconnect the app anytime in the Oura app (Settings → Connected apps) or at cloud.ouraring.com.
